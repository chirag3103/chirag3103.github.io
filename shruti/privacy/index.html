Shruti — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shruti is a raga visualizer for the Mohan Veena and other string instruments on Android. It shows where a raga's notes fall on the fretboard and keyboard, and plays them.

Short version: Shruti has no internet access. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and shows no ads. The only things it saves are your own tunings and where you left off, and those stay on your phone.

Who is responsible

Shruti is made by Chirag Agarwal Labs, an independent developer in Montréal, Québec, Canada.

Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Chirag Agarwal (Chirag Agarwal Labs), developer — apps@agarwalchirag.com

Shruti cannot connect to the internet

Shruti doesn't request Android's internet permission, so the app has no way to send anything off your phone. Everything it needs, including the ragas and the sounds, is built into the app. You can confirm this on your phone under Settings → Apps → Shruti → Permissions, or under "App permissions" on Shruti's Google Play page.

What Shruti handles, and where it stays

What What Shruti does with it Stored? Leaves your phone?
Your saved tunings Keeps the string tunings you name and save for a raga, so you can return to them. Yes, in Shruti's private app storage on your phone No
Where you left off (raga, key, view, strings, recent ragas) Reopens Shruti as you left it. Yes, in Shruti's private app storage on your phone No

Shruti doesn't use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or files, and doesn't ask for them.

What Shruti does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising, and no third-party code that does any of these.
  • No purchases.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There's nothing to share.

Google Play

If you've chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google in your phone's settings, Google Play may give developers anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics. These never include your tunings or anything you do in the app. Google's own privacy policy covers them: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

Permission Why
View network connections Added by the audio library that plays notes. It can't connect to anything, and Shruti doesn't use it.

Android may also list an internal permission named after the app. It keeps Shruti's own messages private and grants nothing to anyone else.

Children

Shruti isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Shruti collects no personal information, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove everything Shruti stores (your saved tunings and where you left off), uninstall Shruti, or go to Settings → Apps → Shruti → Storage → Clear data. For questions or complaints under Québec's Law 25 or any other privacy law, write to apps@agarwalchirag.com. You can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Changes

If a future version of Shruti ever needs to connect to the internet or send data off your phone, for example to download more ragas, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The app will say what's sent and to whom, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
